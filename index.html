Good to know

Still have a question? Call the shop — someone always picks up.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments keep your wait short, especially on Saturday. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is open. Call (347) 555-0186 if you want to know before you leave the house.

How long does a visit take?

A signature cut is about 45 minutes. Color and highlights run longer — we will tell you the time when you book so you are not guessing.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes. Ask for Maya, Jordan, or Priya when you book. If they are booked out, we will offer the next open chair or the next day they are in.

Do you take cards or cash only?

Cards, tap to pay, and cash. Gratuity is welcome and never required to book.
